# OPT service microbenchmark

Two one-decision CPU/GPU development cases per declared wall-clock budget. These are numerical microbenchmarks with a 0.025 m ego sphere and do not constitute complete physical-collider episodes, tracking validation, or comparative safety results.

`summary.json` records source hashes, settings, initial state, startup and service costs, every initialization/solver attempt and deadline availability. NPZ files retain complete candidate-plan and accepted-first-hold states, commands, forces and obstacle predictions. Serialization runs after service timing. No previous plan is installed by warmup.
